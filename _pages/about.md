I am an incoming PhD student in Music Technology at New York University, starting Fall 2026, advised by Prof. [Juan Pablo Bello](https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/people/juan-pablo-bello). I received my M.S. from the Graduate School of AI at KAIST, advised by Prof. [Juhan Nam](https://mac.kaist.ac.kr/~juhan/), and my B.S. in Computer Science from Korea University. My research interests lie in audio generation, sound event detection, and music information retrieval.

Outside of research, I recharge by hiking, traveling, and doing yoga, basically anything that gets me away from a screen and into nature. Music is always in the background, which explains why I ended up studying it.
